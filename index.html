    Style, finish and difficulty
    Click a style to see what it takes for this design. If it looks like too much, step down; if it looks easy, step up. The 3D view, fasteners, steps and shopping list all follow.
    Time is hands-on working time for one person and does not include glue or finish drying. Skill, time and price are rough guides, not promises. Any fastener you picked by hand in the Joints tab is kept.
    Sizes stay the same for every wood: store boards are smaller than their names (a 2x4 is 1 1/2" × 3 1/2"). True rough-sawn boards from a sawmill are a full 2" × 4", so measure yours and adjust if you use them.
    Import a cut list
    Found a free plan online (a bird house, a chair, anything)? Type or paste its cut list here, one piece per line, and the pieces are laid out on the floor next to your design, ready to drag into place. You also get the shopping list, cutting diagrams and price for them straight away.
    Each line: quantity, name, material, length and, for plywood, width. Materials: 1x2 to 1x12, 2x2 to 2x12, 4x4, or plywood 3/4, 1/2, 1/4.
    Plans in PDF or SketchUp files cannot be read directly; the cut list is the part that carries over.

    Start from a furniture preset
    Copy in a row
    Makes evenly spaced copies of the selected pieces, like shelf slats or a row of studs.
    Distance is from the start of one copy to the start of the next. It starts as the size of the selection, which puts the copies edge to edge. Type a minus sign to go the other way.
    Mirror
    Build one side of something, select it, and mirror it across the middle of the design to get the matching other side.
    How to use NominalCAD

    The quick way

    Click Furniture presets, pick something, set your size, and press Create. Then open Build instructions for the shopping list, cut list and steps, and print them.

    Building your own

    Draw a boardPick a material on the left, click Draw a board, and drag on the floor. Hold Shift to stand it upright.
    MoveClick a piece, then drag it. It snaps to other pieces. Shift + drag raises or lowers it. Alt turns snapping off.
    Arrows and ringsA selected piece shows red, green and blue arrows: drag one to slide it in just that direction. Click Rotate to get rings instead, and drag a ring to turn the piece to any angle.
    Box selectHold Shift and drag across empty space to select everything inside the box.
    Copy and mirrorEdit menu, or the buttons in the right panel: Duplicate, Duplicate and drag, Copy in a row (evenly spaced copies) and Mirror (the matching other side).
    Right-clickRight-click a piece for a menu of everything you can do to it. Right-click empty space for view options.
    Hide and see throughH hides the selected pieces, Shift+H shows only them, Alt+H brings everything back. Alt+Z makes the wood see-through. View > Exploded view pulls the pieces apart.
    GroupsSelect several pieces and press Ctrl+G. After that, clicking one selects them all. Ctrl+Shift+G ungroups.
    OverlapsPieces that occupy the same space turn red, with a warning at the bottom of the screen.
    Change lengthClick a piece and drag one of the blue handles on its ends, or type a size in the panel on the right.
    ConnectClick Connect, then the piece to move, then the piece it should meet. The fastener is suggested for you.
    MeasureCtrl + click two pieces. The panel on the right shows the gap between them.
    Look aroundDrag empty space to turn the view, right-drag to slide it, scroll to zoom.

    Keyboard

    Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+YUndo / redo
    Ctrl+S / Ctrl+PSave / print instructions
    Ctrl+D / DeleteDuplicate / delete the selected pieces
    Shift+D / Ctrl+MDuplicate and drag the copy / mirror
    Arrow keysNudge the selected piece; PgUp / PgDn raise and lower it
    M / R / SMove / Rotate / Stretch with the mouse. Then X, Y or Z locks a direction (Z is up), typing a number gives an exact amount, click or Enter keeps it, Esc cancels.
    F / 1 3 7 / HomeZoom to the selection / front, side, top view / fit everything
    D / CDraw a board / Connect two pieces
    EscLeave a tool, or clear the selection
    Sizes are in inches. Type them like 36, 3 1/2 or 4' 6. Store lumber is smaller than its name: a 2x4 is really 1 1/2" × 3 1/2".
