    How to use NominalCAD
    The quick way

    Click Furniture presets (the dark button at the left of the ribbon), pick a design on the left, set your size on the right, and press Create. Every size has a smallest and a largest value: a box turns red when a number is outside them, and a line says what it must be. If your numbers cannot work together, a warning box says what is wrong, why, and what to change, and nothing is built until they can. Then open the Build instructions tab for the shopping list, the cut list and the steps, and press Print there.

    New to woodworking? explains every name the program uses for a piece (apron, cleat, slat) and shows how each joint is made, with a drawing. On the pages, a highlighted word can be clicked for the same thing.

    Where things are
    Tabs
    The numbered tabs along the top are the steps of a project: Design, Cut list & shopping, Build instructions. File, at the far left, has New, My projects, Save, Copy and Paste design as text, Load and Print.
    Ribbon
    The row of commands under the tabs, in groups: Start, Add, Tools, Edit, View and Finish. More at the end of a group opens every command of that group. Display switches between realistic wood and color blocks, and turns on see-through, exploded view and the color key. A command that is gray needs something first, usually a selected piece.
    Panels
    The Pieces list and the Properties of the selected piece float over the 3D view. Drag one by its title bar, roll it up with the dash, or hide it. The two buttons at the right edge of the view bring them back. Properties appears when a piece is selected.
    Status bar
    The bar along the bottom says what to do next with the current tool and shows live sizes while you drag. It also has the overall size, the piece count, a warning when pieces overlap, the Fasteners and Sizes switches, and the Snap size.
    Dialogs
    A window that pops up over the view, such as Furniture presets, Style & finish or Mirror. The dark button on the right does the job, and Enter presses it. Cancel, the X in the corner and Esc all close it without changing anything. Hold the pointer over a box to read a tip about it.
    Beginner and Expert
    The switch at the top right. Beginner shows the three steps a first project needs, and the program picks every screw and joint for you. Expert adds one more tab, Fasteners, after Design: a table of every place two pieces meet, where you can change the screw or joint by hand. Nothing is missing in Beginner: the hardware to buy and which fastener goes where are on the other pages.
    Pages to print
    Every tab after Design is a page to read on screen or print. On those tabs the ribbon holds the commands of the page: Back to design, Print, and Style & finish, which picks the wood, the finish and how fancy the joints are. On the Cut list & shopping tab, type the prices from your store and the total follows.
    Check my design
    In the Tools group, under More (or right-click empty space in the view). It looks for pieces that nothing holds up, boards too long for their load, and anything that could tip or lean over, and says what to change. Every preset passes it as it comes; run it after you change a design by hand or draw your own. These are rule-of-thumb checks, not an engineer's certification.
    Prices
    The prices in the shopping list start as estimates: national averages, not a quote from any store. Update prices asks the national lumber price index how much boards and plywood have moved and moves the estimates; it is the only time the program goes online, and it sends nothing about your design. Stores do not let a program read their prices, so the exact ones are typed by hand: on the Cut list & shopping tab, look an item up at your store and type its price into the box. A price you type is marked "yours", is remembered, and is never changed by an update. Price book lists every price, and Paste prices reads them from lines copied from a receipt.
    Another device
    A design stays in the browser it was made in. To move it, click File, then Copy design as text, and send the text to yourself in an email or a note. On the other device click File, then Paste design from text. This online copy cannot print or download files, so move a design to the copy on your computer this way to print it.
    Building your own
    Draw a board
    Pick a material in the Add group of the ribbon, click Draw a board, and drag on the floor. Hold Shift to stand it upright.
    Move
    Click a piece, then drag it. It snaps to other pieces. Shift + drag raises or lowers it. Alt turns snapping off.
    Arrows and rings
    A selected piece shows red, green and blue arrows: drag one to slide it in just that direction. Click Rotate to get rings instead, and drag a ring to turn the piece to any angle. The colors match the X, Y, Z symbol in the corner of the view.
    Box select
    Hold Shift and drag across empty space to select everything inside the box.
    Copy and mirror
    In the Edit group of the ribbon: Duplicate, Copy in a row (evenly spaced copies) and Mirror (the matching other side). More has Duplicate and drag.
    Right-click
    Right-click a piece for a menu of everything you can do to it. Right-click empty space for view options.
    Hide and see through
    H hides the selected pieces, Shift+H shows only them, Alt+H brings everything back. Alt+Z makes the wood see-through. Display, then Exploded view, pulls the pieces apart. Show me, beside a word under "Words used in this design" on the pages, shows where those pieces are: they turn blue and the rest goes see-through until you click.
    Groups
    Select several pieces and press Ctrl+G. After that, clicking one selects them all. Ctrl+Shift+G ungroups.
    Overlaps
    Pieces that occupy the same space turn red, and a warning appears in the status bar. Click the warning to select them.
    Change length
    Click a piece and drag one of the blue handles on its ends, or type a size in the Properties panel.
    Connect
    Click Connect, then the piece to move, then the piece it should meet. The fastener is suggested for you.
    Measure
    Ctrl + click two pieces. The Properties panel shows the gap between them.
    Import a cut list
    Found a free plan online? Import a cut list, in the Add group, takes its pieces one per line and lays them on the floor beside your design.
    Look around
    Drag empty space to turn the view, right-drag to slide it, scroll to zoom. Fit view, Front, Side and Top are in the View group.
